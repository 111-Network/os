# Layer 9 spec v0.1 review: observation, diagnosis and the floor

**Verdict: amend-then-lock.** Two blockers, both in the incident table of §3.4 and both closed by spec text; no redesign. Judged 2026-11-12, read-only,
against `README.md` §9 and the operating principles, D35, D36, the brief, R00-R07g3, the locked specs and the code. Draft lines are
`9-evolution/README.md:<line>`; `N/`, `NI/`, `A/`, `M/`, `API`, `L0`…`L7` as the draft defines them. Code lines were read at `fa1daeb` (the draft's head);
