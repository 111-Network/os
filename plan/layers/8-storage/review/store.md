**Verdict: amend-then-lock.** Three blockers, each with a local fix; no redesign. Read against `8-storage/README.md` (draft v0.1, 2026-11-11), D34, D36, the
brief, R00-R07g3 and the code at `fa1daeb` (the draft was written at `f0b6806`; `M/store/` citations were also read with `git show f0b6806:`). Draft lines
