# Layer 7 spec v0.1 — panel review, lens C (protocol and custody)

Judge: fable, read-only, 2026-11-02, tree `ec9954b` (mesh111 unchanged since `3bf545b`). Sections judged: §5 (the twelve amendments, the `fwd`
table, the hint TLV, quotas, eviction, evidence), §4.6 as it amends L5 §7.3, §8 carriage and bytes, §11 frames, the `forwarding` reason in
§13, and the custody/forwarding cases of §14. Every `M/`, `N/`, `ID/`, `NI/`, `L5`, `L2` and `API` line the draft cites in those sections
was re-read; all of them resolve to the text the draft describes except where a finding below says otherwise.

**Verdict: amend-then-lock.** The shape (single-copy push, `fwd` rows, the hint TLV, the hand-off API) matches D30 F4/F5 and the code;
nothing needs a redesign. Two normative holes must close before the lock: the gate text contradicts itself on when a no-hint put is
answered, and a forward row that no target will ever claim has no ending, so one origin can fill a hub's Transit space for good.

## Blockers

| id | where | what | fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | §5.2 (no-hint branch), §5.5, §5.8, §5.9 `Rejected`, §14 "Custody starvation" | **A forward row nobody claims lives forever, and the Transit quota is one pool.** A `fwd = 1/2/3` row stays `committed = 0` (step 4 "never", `M/store/prune.rs:32-38`) until the next custodian's ack; when every pair answers `REJECT_UNKNOWN_DEST`, when the carrier's far gateway rejects (§5.9 `Rejected`: "stays protected with the reason"), or when the only remaining target is a carrier the item does not fit, there is no ack, no TTL, no clock and no cancel that reaches a hub. `quota.transit` is global, so one member on any leaf (or a compromised leaf hub, whose gateway holds its `fwd = 2` rows unchecked) fills 128 MiB and every later forward put in the star gets `REJECT_FULL`. Those protected bytes also count in `live_bytes` (`admit_inner`, `:818-833`), so at the 512 MiB watermark member custody is refused too: "member puts are unaffected" (§5.8, §14) is not true in the limit. | Give a forward row a terminal state and the quota an origin share: (a) a row every reachable target refused (every `tcp`/`gateway` pair answered `REJECT_UNKNOWN_DEST` on an attributed connection, or the carrier returned `REJECT_UNKNOWN_DEST`/`Rejected` for it, or the item fits no remaining target) is marked refused — `committed = 1` with a `refused` count in `FabricStatus` and the hub's fabric view — and becomes step-1 evictable; the member keeps `relayed` (D30 Q18 accepted that; say so in §5.6 as the honest limit, sweep-before-ack being v1.1); (b) a per-origin share inside Transit (per member and per pair, e.g. `quota.transit / 8`), over which that origin's forward puts get `REJECT_FULL` while others are admitted; (c) state in §5.8 and §14 that protected Transit rows count toward the high watermark and the owner's cap bounds that exposure. Owner acknowledgement (it touches the evidence rule). G9 gains "every pair refuses ⇒ the row is refused, evictable and counted; a second origin still forwards". |
| C2 | §5.2 "from a member", third clause vs §5.5 and §5.9 | **The gate contradicts itself on when a no-hint put is answered.** §5.2 reads "no hint ⇒ probe the `tcp` `peer` and `leaf` pairs (§5.5), then the `gateway` pair, then the `carrier` pairs, else `REJECT_UNKNOWN_DEST`", i.e. the probe decides the receipt; §5.5 says every pair refusing "leaves the row protected, offered again at the next paired-hub link-up", i.e. the row was held and acked first. `on_custody_put` (`M/fabric.rs:2824-2862`) is a synchronous arm of the single actor and answers `CUSTODY_ACK` before returning; it cannot await a pair's round trip, and D30 puts sweep-before-ack in v1.1. A builder must guess which; the two readings give different `relayed` semantics. | Rewrite the no-hint clause: "no hint ⇒ a target exists (any `tcp` or `gateway` pair; or a `carrier` pair and the put fits its bound) ⇒ hold `fwd = 1`, answer `ACCEPTED`, then probe per §5.5 (event-driven, after the ack); no target at all ⇒ `REJECT_UNKNOWN_DEST`". Make the hinted clause match (a hint matching no pair ⇒ hold when a `gateway` or fitting `carrier` pair exists, else reject). Add one sentence to §5.6: the first hub's `ACCEPTED` means "held and will be offered", never "the dest's hub was found". |

## Majors

| C3 | §4.6 step 2, §5.1 table, §5.7 (b) | **Roles are picked independently on each side; loop freedom assumes they are complementary.** The `fwd` table is loop-free only when a pair is `leaf` on one side and `gateway` on the other, or `peer` on both, and when every `leaf` is a `tcp` pair (row 3's "final put" and the §5.5 probe both need a connection). Two hubs that both mark each other `leaf` make each other's `fwd = 2` rows carrier-bound; a far gateway whose `leaf` pair is itself `via = carrier` has a row-3 target it may not use ("never a carrier again") and no rule says what happens; a mismarked leaf's `ACCEPTED` commits the gateway's row while its own sticks (C1). | Negotiate the role in the bundle: the shown bundle carries the offering side's proposed role; the scanning side's pick must be the complement (`peer`↔`peer`, `leaf`↔`gateway`) or `POST /relay/pair` answers 409 `pair_role_mismatch`; a `carrier` pair's role is `peer` only; a `leaf` pair is `tcp` only; a forward row from a pair whose recorded role conflicts with the row's `fwd` is refused `REJECT_UNKNOWN_DEST`. G9 adds the asymmetric case. |
| C4 | §5.1, §5.3 amendment 10, §17 | **A hub's own messages cannot reach a paired hub's members.** `route()` knows only `home_hub` (`M/fabric.rs:1821-1841`); the hub's own outbox never enters its custody table, and amendment 10 sets no `home_hub` on pairing. An always-on laptop that is a hub (`custody_opt_in`, `holds_custody`, `:356-360`) with no home hub of its own sees `queued` for every contact on another island, which breaks "lets delivery cross islands" for the very node that carries it. | State it in §5.1 and §17: in v1 a hub's own messages reach other islands only through its own home hub, if it has one; an own-send route into its own forward table (`Route::Forward`, local, no wire) is v1.1 beside the own-send carrier route (D30 Q20). If the owner wants it in v1 it is a thirteenth Layer 5 change and §19 must list it. |
| C5 | §5.3 amendment 2 (`L5:386-389`) | **The replacement text leaves the locked scope sentence contradicted, and misstates the state.** Amendment 2 replaces only "— cross-hub delivery needs A+ (§21)."; the preceding "a hub accepts custody only for its own members (and only from members); store-and-forward therefore works between members of one hub or over a direct link" (`L5:386-388`) still stands and forward rows (custody for non-members, from paired hubs) contradict it. The new tail "otherwise the message stays `queued`, reason `hub_not_yours`" is wrong against the code: with the hub reachable the put is sent and `REJECT_UNKNOWN_DEST` leaves it `sent` (`M/fabric.rs:3053-3061`; `API:903` says both). | Replace the whole scope sentence: "a hub holds custody for its members, from its members; with a paired hub or a carrier it also holds forward rows for non-member dests from its members, its leaf hubs and its carrier (Layer 7 §5); a message to a contact whose hub is not yours and not reachable through a pair stays `queued` (your hub unreachable) or `sent` (your hub answered `REJECT_UNKNOWN_DEST`), reason `hub_not_yours`." |
| C6 | §5.9 `Taken` paragraph, §6.2 flush | **Re-post amplification through the carrier.** "A posted row with no ack is posted again at a later flush, after a drain found no ack for it" re-posts the whole un-acked backlog on every flush: every debounced netlink change, every `Forward` event, every "Flush now". With N rows waiting on a far gateway that flushes once a day, each flush posts N duplicates, each answered by a `DUPLICATE` ack; this burns the metered cap (§12), the server's 60-burst/5 s limit and any per-day cap, and is not "bounded by flushes" in any useful sense. | A posted row is re-posted only when a drain has returned a frame from the far hub newer than the post (proof the far hub flushed after it) and still no ack for it, or on "Flush now" and on a stub/server restart detected by a `since` miss; count `reposts` per pair in `RelayStatus`; G10 asserts one post per row per far-hub round. |
| C7 | §5.9 `forward_take` / `flush` signatures | **The ack frames' lifecycle through a carrier is unspecified.** `forward_take` returns `CUSTODY_ACK`s with `custody_seq = None`, but `flush` reports `Vec<(u64, ForwardDone)>`, so an ack's fate cannot be returned; nothing says whether a taken ack is consumed, re-taken after `Retry`, or dropped. Without a rule the far hub's row never commits (ack lost) or the ack is posted forever. | Say: acks are consumed on take and never retried; a lost ack heals because the far hub re-posts the put (C6 rule) and the next `carrier_inbound` answers `DUPLICATE`, a fresh ack. Change `flush` to `Vec<(Option<u64>, ForwardDone)>` or state that `None` frames are fire-and-forget. |
| C8 | §5.3 amendment 6 (`M/store/rows.rs:31-56,92-105`), §5.8 | **The store changes behind `Class::Transit` are under-cited; followed literally they count forward rows against members.** `class_bytes(Class::Custody)` sums the whole `custody` table (`M/store/prune.rs:484-490`) and `evictable` labels every committed custody row `Class::Custody` (`:228-245`); neither is in the amendment, and `custody_put` (`rows.rs:1010-1030`) admits every row under `Class::Custody`. Built as cited, the Transit quota is never charged and member custody's quota absorbs strangers' rows: the opposite of §5.8's promise. | Amendment 6 names `prune.rs:484-509` and `:228-245`: `class_bytes(Custody)` = `WHERE fwd = 0`, `class_bytes(Transit)` = `WHERE fwd != 0`, `evictable` assigns `Class::Transit` to `fwd != 0`; `is_refusable` and `has_quota` include Transit; `custody_put` takes `fwd` and admits under the row's class. |
| C9 | §5.3 amendment 10 vs §4.6 settings block, §5.2 | **Two shapes for the pairing record, and the fabric's lacks what the gate needs.** mesh111's `PairedHub {node_id, leaf, gateway}` carries no `via`; §5.2 picks targets by `tcp` vs `carrier` and §5.5 probes only `tcp` pairs, so the fabric cannot apply its own gate from that struct. ## Notes

- What is good: the twelve amendments are real and additive as claimed; every mesh111 line in §5 resolves (gate `2824-2862`, `CUSTODY_WRAP` 120,
  `pkt_ref` UNIQUE, step-4 protection, `evictable` by size × age, `on_custody_ack` home-hub-only, `on_enroll_ok` writing `home_hub` and a cursor,
  `rebuild_home_hub`); the C5 correction (commit on the far hub's `CUSTODY_ACK`, not the carrier's accept) is right and is what keeps `relayed` honest.
- The `fwd` table is loop-free with complementary roles (C3); with them the longest path is the stated five hops and `pkt_ref` ends any bounce.
- Byte tables check: hint TLV 11 B; `CUSTODY_ACK` ≈ 110 B; ntfy batch 3,072 raw, `max_frame` 3,068, item ≤ 2,937 B; beacon 72 B; attestation 202 B ≤ 255.
- The epoch carriage (ext TLV on `ENROLL_OK` and mailbox answers) is right given `M/mailbox.rs:16-18`; nothing in v1 reads a clock.
- No sentence outside the twelve contradicts locked L5 except the scope sentence amendment 2 half-replaces (C5); the `tcp-lan` seam is correctly flagged D1.
- Watch in the build: `class_bytes`/`evictable` with `fwd` (C8); the forwarder's single-actor shape (the probe is a put sent after the ack, C2);
  the re-post rule (C6) and the ack lifecycle (C7) in `carrier.sh` G10; the role check at `POST /relay/pair` (C3).
- The V4 migration runs on every node's store at first open (one `ALTER TABLE`, user_version 4); a rollback to a Layer 6 build refuses the
  store with `SchemaTooNew`, exactly as V3 did at Layer 6 (`6-transport/README.md:136`). Record it in `TASKS.md`, not a finding.
