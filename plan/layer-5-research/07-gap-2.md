# Layer 5 gap G2: v1 delivery scope (keys, contacts, custody, "relayed")

Scout run 2026-10-14. Fetched facts win over memory. Lines marked (unverified) were not confirmed from a fetched source.

## 1. Summary

- **A `node1…` string cannot be sealed to.** L2 derives the X25519 key independently of the Ed25519 NodeId (identity/README.md section 2), so a sender also needs the peer's signed announce (137 B). The genesis announce already is a self-authenticating contact card; no new L2 artifact is needed.
- **v1 emits announces on demand, never on a timer**: in a contact card, on link-up, inside a first message. This sidesteps the announce rate policy L2 section 13 handed to L6/L7, avoids periodic fsyncs of the announce counter, and works for Termux phones that cannot receive multicast (devices.md section 2.2).
- **Custody in v1 is directed, not routed.** A node's enrolled home hub is the custodian; hubs may replicate among themselves. Epidemic, spray-and-wait, PRoPHET and MaxProp wait for mobility and announces that v1 lacks.
- **Nothing is reusable as a library.** dtn7-rs routing is a tokio actor over `bp7` bundle types; the algorithms are small and published. No other maintained OSI-licensed crate surfaced (one search; negative result, unverified).
- **"relayed" needs an authenticated custody receipt.** Proposed `CUSTODY_ACK`: a sealed reply bound to the exact packet bytes, domain tag `111/custody/v1`. Meshtastic and MeshCore cannot supply one.
- **Pick: option A** (direct links + hub mailbox + card/QR contacts), hub-to-hub replication as A+. Four small L2 amendments, one L4 copy tweak, only the `tcp-lan` adapter from L6, nothing from L7.

## 3. Findings

1. **Keys are not derivable from NodeId.** Signing key at `m/111'/0'/0'`, ECDH key at `m/111'/0'/1'`; public keyset 64 B (identity/README.md section 2). `node1…` carries 32 B. The 10-char label is about 50 bits and display-only (section 7), so contact trust uses the full `node1…` or a card, never the label.
2. **The announce is already the card**: `sign_pubkey`, `enc_pubkey`, counter, 0-32 B appdata, signature under `111/announce/v1`, 137-169 B (section 6.2). Section 13's "v1 emits no announces" only justifies deferring L6/7's start; `111id` already encodes and verifies them.
3. **SecureJoin's lesson is one out-of-band message**: the invite carries the fingerprint plus contact info, the rest runs over an attacker-controlled channel. https://securejoin.readthedocs.io/en/latest/summary.html. Its QR is `fingerprint&v&i&s&a` (invite number, auth token, address) and the module is bound to `Context`. https://github.com/chatmail/core/blob/main/src/securejoin.rs. Mapping: our full `node1…` is the fingerprint; a one-time enrollment token is `i`/`s`.
4. **Peers' announce behaviour.** MeshCore adverts carry a unix timestamp. https://raw.githubusercontent.com/meshcore-dev/MeshCore/main/docs/payloads.md. Meshtastic NodeInfo defaults to every 3 h. https://meshtastic.org/docs/overview/mesh-algo/. Reticulum caps announce processing at 2% of interface bandwidth, queued by hop count; the manual gives no periodic interval. https://reticulum.network/manual/understanding.html.
5. **dtn7-rs routing is not a library seam.** `RoutingAgent` exposes only `channel()`; work is `RoutingCmd` messages (`SenderForBundle(BundlePack, …)`, `Notify`) over tokio mpsc; routers: Epidemic, Flooding, Sink, External, SprayAndWait, Static. https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dtn7/dtn7-rs/master/core/dtn7/src/routing/mod.rs. Default dependencies include sled, axum, hyper, mdns-sd. https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dtn7/dtn7-rs/master/core/dtn7/Cargo.toml. Last release 0.21.0, 2024-03-27. https://crates.io/api/v1/crates/dtn7.
6. **Algorithm needs.** PRoPHET ages predictabilities by local time units, so it needs a local timer; exchange is RIB plus bundle offer/response. https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc6693.txt. Epidemic uses summary-vector anti-entropy and suffers under small buffers. https://arxiv.org/pdf/1210.0965. Spray-and-wait caps copies. https://www.cs.uoi.gr/~epap/L05/downloads/lt/spyropoulos-multi.pdf. Lens 4's `msg_id = (sender, stream, seq)` with prefix-plus-ranges (about 24-48 B per stream, derived) is already a compact summary vector.
7. **Prior-art custody signals.** LXMF: propagated sends end in SENT; DELIVERED is set only for opportunistic and direct sends. https://raw.githubusercontent.com/markqvist/LXMF/master/LXMF/LXMessage.py. Briar Mailbox: QR pairing, Tor. https://briarproject.org/news/2023-briar-mailbox-released/. Its test plan reportedly shows one tick on upload to the mailbox, two on ack (secondary, via search; wiki returned 403). https://code.briarproject.org/briar/briar/-/wikis/message-upload-both-x-and-y-have-mailboxes. Meshtastic: broadcasts are acked implicitly by any rebroadcast; a DM is acked only by its recipient; no intermediate acceptance signal documented (mesh-algo URL above). S&F search snippets: https://meshtastic.org/docs/configuration/module/store-and-forward-module/. BPv7 custody moved to the BIBE draft. https://www.ietf.org/archive/id/draft-ietf-dtn-bibect-03.txt.
8. **LAN multicast announces do not reach phones.** Termux has no multicast receive (devices.md section 2.2, D12); phones use unicast TCP to a hub anyway (D17 #4).
9. **Lens 5 already has custody refusal**: undelivered outbound is never pruned; a full store refuses new custody and shows `store_full`. The receipt needs a matching REJECT_FULL status.

## 4. Recommendation

### 4.1 What v1 contains (any option)

**Contacts.** One L5 `contacts` table: full `enc_pubkey`, `trust` (`oob` | `tofu`), local alias, home-hub hint. Three ways in:

- **Card:** the genesis announce as a binary QR, or base32 text (about 220 chars; bech32 is checksum-sound only near 90 chars, section 7). Works with zero connectivity.
- **`node1…` only:** typed or scanned (63 chars). The node fetches the announce over any link and verifies it against the expected NodeId (the SecureJoin shape). Messages stay `queued`, reason "waiting for their key".
- **INTRO:** the first message to a never-contacted peer carries the sender's announce (137 B, inner, signed under `111/msg/v1`). The receiver files a *request* with `trust = tofu`; the user accepts or ignores.

**appdata v1 (4 B).** `[ver=1][roles u16][reserved]`: bit 0 mailbox, bit 1 always-on hub, bit 2 LoRa-attached; optional 8 B truncated home-hub id (locator only). L7 may append TLVs.

**Mailbox protocol.** Frames are L2 sealed packets on a length-prefixed TCP stream, so the same frames later ride LoRa. No Noise or TLS in v1 (Noise IK stays an L2 section 6.6 SHOULD; Rust Noise stacks are unaudited, section 11). Control kinds are sealed to the hub's `enc_pubkey`:

- `CUSTODY_PUT{dest NodeId, packet}`: the double seal costs 56 B, irrelevant on TCP.
- `CUSTODY_ACK`: below.
- `MAILBOX_FETCH{cursor}`: the hub's local insertion sequence, no clock.
- `MAILBOX_COMMIT{cursor}`: recipient stored everything up to it; the hub marks those items evict-first.

**Enrollment.** Client and hub swap cards plus a one-time token shown on the hub (the L4 section 6.9 pair-code ceremony reused for nodes). A hub refuses PUT from unenrolled nodes; that is the owner's access list, not a rate policy.

**Retry.** No timers: retry on link and contact events; keep the sender's copy until `delivered`.

### 4.2 Custody receipt and the four states

`CUSTODY_ACK` is a sealed reply of kind `CUSTODY`, MAC'd with the lens-4 `pair_mac` under context `111/custody/v1`:

`status (ACCEPTED | DUPLICATE | REJECT_FULL | REJECT_UNENROLLED) | custody_seq (varint) | pkt_ref = BLAKE2b-128("111/custody/v1" ‖ exact sealed packet bytes)`

About 98 B sealed (computed). Hashing the exact bytes forces the custodian to hold them. An optional transferable form signed under `111/custody-sig/v1` (64 B) is available on request.

| L4 state | Evidence in v1 | Note |
|---|---|---|
| queued | in outbox; no link, or keys missing | reason shown |
| sent | frame written to a link | LoRa: modem accepted TX |
| relayed | authenticated `CUSTODY_ACK` ACCEPTED from a mailbox-role node | custodian's promise, not delivery; show its label |
| delivered | recipient's `kind=ACK` (lens 4) | only this releases the sender's copy |

An overheard repeater echo is **not** `relayed`; show it as detail on `sent`. Suggested L4 copy: "relayed: a mailbox node saved it and will hold it for them."

### 4.3 Scope options

| Option | Scope | L6 | L7 | L2 | Trade-off |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A (pick)** | Direct links + home-hub mailbox + card/QR/INTRO, on-demand announces | `tcp-lan` (built with L5, lens 1) | none | amendments 1-4 | Smallest, deterministic, fits phones; no multi-hop discovery. |
| **A+** | A + hub-to-hub epidemic over configured peers (summary vector = lens-4 ranges) | same | none | same | Cheap on always-on hubs; storage multiplies; replicas evict first. |
| B | LAN multicast announces + epidemic among all nodes | multicast adapter | presence, announce rate policy | periodic emission, per-announce fsync | Phones excluded (finding 8); strangers TOFU-pinned; flash wear. |
| C | A + announce-as-routing across hubs | `kiss-modem`, hint use | announce flooding, Cat-1 verify policy, mesh-epoch | all of B | Dynamic custody choice but pulls most of L7 forward. |

Spray-and-wait fits later: a copy budget of at most 7 fits the three spare bits of the lens-4 L6 shim (computed). PRoPHET and MaxProp wait for mobile multi-hub traffic and a decided local timer.

**L2 amendments for A:**

1. Section 13: "v1 emits no *unsolicited or periodic* announces; on-demand announces are allowed."
2. User-added contacts exempt from LRU eviction, or the L5 `contacts` table is authoritative for `enc_pubkey` (L2's 256-entry store becomes a cache). Otherwise an evicted contact is unreachable until a new announce.
3. Register the appdata v1 layout; L7 owns later extension.
4. Register `111/custody/v1` and `111/custody-sig/v1` in the domain-tag registry.

## 5. Open questions for the owner

1. Scope: A, A+, or another cut? (A+ can follow A without redesign.)
2. Should a hub accept custody from unenrolled senders (open mailbox, bounded by `store_full`), or stay enrolled-only (recommended)?
3. Is INTRO-as-message-request acceptable first contact, or must every contact come from a card or `node1…`?
4. Should `relayed` strictly mean "a mailbox-role node confirmed durable custody", with repeater echoes only as `sent` detail?
5. Who may be custodians: hubs and always-on laptops (recommended), or opt-in phones too?
6. Approve L2 amendments 1-4, including who owns appdata extension after v1.
7. Sealed frames over TCP for the hub link now, Noise IK later: agreed?
8. Should a recipient with two hubs get replicated copies (A+) in v1?
