## Drafting notes for the lawyer (remove before release)

1. **Scope: noncommercial organizations (open point).** PolyForm's "Noncommercial Organizations" section allows charitable organizations, educational institutions, public research
   organizations, public safety or health organizations, environmental protection organizations and government institutions, not only individuals. The owner's sentence says "personal use". This
   draft keeps the PolyForm scope because it fits the humanitarian aim. Striking that section would make it personal-only. Part B2 removes armed forces from the government-institution case.
2. **Armed forces doing relief (open point).** B3 lets civilian emergency, disaster-relief and humanitarian work count as permitted even when Armed Forces carry it out. Left as drafted; the owner and the lawyer should confirm it.
3. **Definition edges:** non-state armed groups, private military companies, and a civilian firm whose product is later used by Armed Forces. B1(2) is limited to work done for Armed Forces.
4. **Contributor terms (open point).** Offering commercial agreements and changing terms later needs a contributor license or copyright assignment. None exists yet.
5. **Earlier declarations.** Until 2026-10-14 the Cargo manifests said `MIT OR Apache-2.0`. If any copy was ever distributed under that statement, the lawyer should advise on those copies.
6. **Packaging.** Because Part B is not part of PolyForm, the SPDX value for our code is not `PolyForm-Noncommercial-1.0.0`. The manifests use `license-file`; if an SPDX string is ever needed, use `LicenseRef-111network`.
